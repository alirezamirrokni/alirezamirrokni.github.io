## Research Interests

My current research focuses on building principled and reliable machine learning systems, ranging from theoretical foundations to modern language and vision-language models. My recent work spans several areas:

- **Theoretical machine learning and statistics**
- **Optimization and resource-aware learning**
- **Interpretability and causal analysis of LLMs and VLMs**
- **Multimodal and multilingual reasoning**
