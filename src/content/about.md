I am a final-year undergraduate student in Computer Engineering at **Sharif University of Technology**.

I am broadly interested in the foundations and reliability of machine learning systems, including statistical learning, optimization, interpretability, and multimodal reasoning. Last summer, I was a research intern at the [Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA)](https://ist.ac.at/en/research/mondelli-group/), where I worked with [Prof. Marco Mondelli](https://marcomondelli.com/). Before that, I had the privilege of working with [Prof. Hossein Esfandiari](https://sites.google.com/view/hossein-esfandiari/home). I was also a research intern at [Qatar Computing Research Institute (QCRI)](https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/qcri) with [Prof. Ehsaneddin Asgari](https://llm-lab.org/ehsan/).

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on building principled and reliable machine learning systems, ranging from theoretical foundations to modern language and vision-language models. My recent work spans several areas:

- **Theoretical machine learning and statistics**
- **Optimization and resource-aware learning**
- **Interpretability and causal analysis of LLMs and VLMs**
- **Multimodal and multilingual reasoning**
